Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 19, 2026

Information we collect

When you create an account or enroll, we may collect your name, email address, phone number, account credentials, payment reference, and learning progress.

How we use information

We use this information to create and secure your account, process enrollment payments, provide course access, save learning progress, communicate about your account, and improve the platform.

Payments

Payments are processed by Paystack. Zentrix Academy does not store your full card details. Paystack may process payment information according to its own privacy policy and terms.

Cookies and local storage

We use necessary cookies for authenticated sessions. The application may also use browser local storage to remember your last lesson on that device. Do not use shared devices without signing out.

Data security

We use reasonable technical safeguards, including password hashing, secure session cookies, and access controls. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

Data retention and requests

We retain account and learning information while it is needed to provide the service and meet operational or legal requirements. To request account information or deletion, contact the academy through its official support channel.

Policy updates

We may update this policy as the platform changes. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new revision date.
